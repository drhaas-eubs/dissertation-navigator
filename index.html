Bachelor track

BBA Dissertation

The Bachelor figures in one place: 14,000 words, 50–80 references, 70% pass mark, and the 70% written / 30% oral defence weighting — with the same hard gates as the MBA.
Open the BBA dissertation →
Three things are non-negotiable at both levels. A signed Ethics Discussion and Agreement before any data is collected; a mandatory AI Annex (PowerPoint, one screenshot per source, working OneDrive link); and Harvard referencing throughout, with in-text citations and the reference list matching both ways. Everything else is quality; these are gates.
